# Thursday — Sharing

**Goal:** assemble a Space so the whole team works from the same context, then
produce two outputs from one shared place: a chart for the researcher and a
sourced brief for the Extension agent.

There are no new data files to download for Thursday — it combines what you
already built earlier in the week. This folder is instructions only.

## Put these in one Space

- **Monday's weather file** — `azmet_daily_yuma_pinal_2021_2026.csv`, uploaded
  to the Space.
- **Tuesday's paper library** — the S3 knowledge base, *linked* to the Space.
- **Tuesday's research report** — download the Quick Research report as a PDF
  and upload that PDF to the Space (a report cannot be added to a Space
  directly).

> The Wednesday method card stays attached to the **agent** as its reference
> document; it is not uploaded to the Space. If you want the Thursday drafting
> to use Wednesday's conclusion, run it with the Wednesday agent (which already
> has the card) and point it at this Space, or paste the key numbers into the
> prompt.

## Sharing notes

- A Space has two roles: **Owner** (add, share, delete) and **Viewer** (ask
  questions, download). An owner can make others co-owners.
- **Sharing the Space does not share a linked knowledge base.** Share the S3
  paper library with each teammate separately, as a Viewer, or their answers
  cannot cite the papers. Files *uploaded* to the Space are visible to everyone
  in it.

## Two prompts to run (chart first)

Run them as two separate requests — asking for both in one prompt is riskier.

**1. The chart (for the researcher):**

> Bar chart of total reference ET from January 1 to September 30 for each year,
> 2021 to 2026, at Yuma Valley (az02). One bar per year, 2026 highlighted.
> Include days flagged needs_review.

Expected yearly totals: 65.4, 66.9, 62.4, 63.7, 64.5, **68.3** (2026 highest).

**2. The brief (for the Extension agent):**

> Draft a one-page brief for Pinal cotton growers on this season's heat and
> water and what a water cut next year could mean. Cite the station and dates
> for each number.

Every number in the brief should trace back to an AZMet station and date range,
or to a published source — and a person reviews it before it goes to any grower.
